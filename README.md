## The problem
Most gift ideas are generic because the giver doesn't know enough about the receiver, or doesn't know how to turn what they *do* know into a good idea.

## How it works

| Step | |
|---|---|
| **1. Tell us about them** | Share hobbies, personality and quirks through a smart form or a voice recording |
| **2. Chat with the AI** | An "investigator" AI spots gaps in what you know and asks targeted questions, Akinator style |
| **3. Get matches** | Curated recommendations, each with a match score, the reasoning behind it, and purchase or DIY links |
| **4. Add the final touch** | The AI writes a personalised note. For handmade gifts, it suggests YouTube tutorials matched to your skill level |

## Features
- **AI persona engine:** deep personality profiling from casual conversation
- **Akinator-style chat:** uncovers preferences you didn't realise you knew
- **Collaboration mode:** group gifting with no duplicate recommendations
- **Success predictions:** a match score for every gift
- **Sneaky intel:** subtle questions you can ask the receiver without giving the surprise away
